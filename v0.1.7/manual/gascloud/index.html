Gas Cloud

A simple SPH gas cloud sampled on a Cartesian grid clipped to a sphere of radius R. Velocities are drawn from a Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution at temperature T.

Example

The particle mass per cell is set to $\rho_0 \cdot R^2 / r^2 \cdot L_x L_y L_z$. ParticleMass is only the mean particle mass used to compute the thermal velocity (vmean); it is not the cell mass stored in each particle.

See GasCloud, generate(::GasCloud) and gridpoints in the Library reference for full signatures.
