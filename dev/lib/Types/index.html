Types

All initial-condition generators are subtypes of InitialConditionConfig. Use the generate dispatcher to produce a StructArray of particles from any of these.

Initial-condition configurations

AstroIC.PlummerStarCluster — Type

  • collection::Collection

  • NumSamples::Any: amount of particles

  • VirialRadius::Any

  • TotalMass::Any: mass are seperated equally to all particles

  • model::Any: gravity model [ Newton | MOND ]
