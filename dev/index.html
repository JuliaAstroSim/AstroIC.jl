AstroIC.jl

Generates initial conditions for astrophysical simulations.

Source code: https://github.com/JuliaAstroSim/AstroIC.jl

Package feature

Initial-condition generators for the standard components of a galaxy-like simulation:

  • Spherical cluster: PlummerStarCluster
  • Galactic disk: ExponentialDisc (with optional central hole)
  • Galactic bulge: Bulge
  • SPH gas cloud: GasCloud
  • Generic spherical system from a mass shell: SphericalSystem
  • Solar system snapshot: solarsystem

Particle-system helpers:

  • Position & velocity translation: addpos, addvel
  • Centering & frame setting: setpos, setvel
  • Cartesian grid generation: gridpoints
  • Thermal speed: vmean

Real-world observational data loaders (pre-bundled with the package):

  • Milky Way satellite galaxies
  • Ultra-faint dwarf galaxies
  • SPARC late- and early-type galaxy rotation curves
  • Milky Way rotation curves (Eilers 2019, Mróz 2019, Wang 2021)
  • Massive dwarf rotation curves (Cooke 2022)
